Questions people ask

How long does it take?

Small fixes, like a security warning or a new email address, are usually done the same day. A new website is usually live within one to two weeks.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. I do all of it, explain anything you want to know in plain English, and only ask for what I really need.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. I can fix what's wrong, make it work on phones or give it a refresh, keeping your wording and look if you like them.

Can you look after it afterwards?

Yes. I can keep it secure, renewed and up to date, so you don't have to think about it.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay. We keep going until you're happy, and you only pay at that point.
