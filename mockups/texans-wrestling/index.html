Parents ask

Good questions

My child has never wrestled. Can they join?

Absolutely. Our main goal is to have fun while learning a new sport, and fundamentals are at the heart of every practice.

What ages do you coach?

Boys and girls from 4 to 18, matched by age and weight so every match is fair.

Which styles do you teach?

All three: Folkstyle, Freestyle and Greco-Roman.

Where and when is practice?
